About

One counter,
five kitchens.

Mr. Chopsticks has been cooking on Scripture Street since 1985. The menu pulls from China, Thailand, Vietnam, Japan and India, and the kitchen builds its dishes around fresh herbs, raw vegetables and a light hand with seasoning rather than heavy sauce.

The dining room is plain and comfortable, there’s seating outside, and there’s no reservation book. You walk in, you order, you eat. Vegan options run through the whole menu, not just a corner of it.

What they cook

Built to feed you
for under twenty.

The menu is long. Here’s the shape of it. The full list, with current prices, lives on the restaurant’s own menu page.

Sushi

Rolls made with the rice outside or inside, plus cuts of eel, salmon, scallop, shrimp, tuna, yellowtail and smoked salmon.

Stir-fry & rice

Wok dishes, fried rice and noodle plates, most with a choice of protein and white, brown or noodle base.

Grilled plates

Protein grilled with vegetables over steamed or fried rice.

Soups

Small and large bowls, including coconut-and-lime chicken soup with mushroom, scallion and cilantro.

Salads & sides

Meal-sized salads, cucumber salad, stir-fried green beans, steamed vegetables and vegetable tempura.

Vegan

Plant-based options across the menu rather than a single token section.

Prices change, so this page doesn’t list them. Google diners report spending $10–20 per person.
